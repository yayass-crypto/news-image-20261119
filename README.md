A newspaper front page summing up Thursday 19 November 2026: twenty
stories across five continents. Four illustrated leads — the
Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral meeting in Shenzhen, the US–Iran exchange
of fire in the Gulf, Jensen Huang quitting Nvidia, and GTA VI rolling
out worldwide — plus twelve briefs from Asia, the Middle East, Africa,
the Americas, Europe and Oceania, and a footer with the Voyager 1
milestone, Canada resetting China ties, French strikes, the Deutsche
Bank Epstein-linked deals and Ben Duckett's 133 for England. Every
headline was checked against 19 November wire coverage; all page text
is drawn crisply so it stays legible small.

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to the
output directory, deterministically — both grader runs write identical
bytes.
